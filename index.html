01 / About

Trust, language
& human context.

I am a Lecturer at East Delta University and a Computer Science graduate of Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology.

My research spans privacy-preserving identity, decentralized data recovery, and sentiment analysis across Bangla dialects. In my undergraduate thesis, I explored how users can verify the trustworthiness of credential verifiers without compromising their privacy.

Across these projects, I bring together methods from distributed systems and machine learning to address practical problems—from safeguarding data to recognizing the nuances of regional language. Alongside research, I teach undergraduate theory and laboratory courses.

02 / Research interests

Three connected lines of inquiry.

i.

Privacy & digital identity

Self-sovereign identity, verifier trust, and privacy-preserving credential verification using federated approaches and zero-knowledge proofs.

ii.

Resilient decentralized systems

Blockchain-based backup and recovery, encrypted distributed storage, data integrity, and multi-party restoration.

iii.

Language-aware machine learning

Bangla dialect and sentiment analysis, hybrid neural models, and transformer-based approaches to language tasks such as humor intensity regression.

A Hybrid Deep Learning Approach for Dialect Specific Sentiment Analysis of Bangla Regional Linguistics

I. Samiha, S. Chowdhury, N. Mobassara, and M. I. Uddin Forkan
